# Work Done in the Assignment

• I imported the Superstore dataset and created `customers`, `orders`, and `products` tables.

• I used `SELECT DISTINCT` to remove duplicate records.

• I used subqueries to analyze sales and find the highest sales orders.

• I used CTEs to calculate customer-wise total sales and simplify queries.

• I used window functions to rank customers and assign row numbers.

• I used JOINs to combine data from multiple tables.

• I generated customer sales insights such as top customers, bottom customers, one-time customers, above-average customers, and highest order values.
